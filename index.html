About The Practice

Your neighborhood clinic since 1999

Westchester Chiropractic Healthcare, P.C. has been caring for the Mount Vernon community for over two decades. Dr. Ciccone and his team of associates and specialists treat every patient with the attention and respect they deserve.

From everyday aches to accident and injury recovery, we bring chiropractic, physical therapy, and pain management together under one roof so your care stays simple and connected.

  • Our patients are our family. You are treated as a person, never a number.

  • Proudly serving Mount Vernon and the surrounding Westchester area.
